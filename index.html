Featured research

Advanced Science · 2026

Ouroboros

Ouroboros addresses a persistent divide in AI-driven drug discovery: representations optimized for prediction are rarely reusable for molecular design. By organizing chemical space with fingerprint, conformational-space, and pharmacophore relationships, it supports property prediction, virtual screening, and property-guided generation within one representation. This creates a common basis for hit discovery, lead optimization, and multi-target design.

Molecular representation

First author

GeminiMol

Finding functional similarity beyond shared 2D scaffolds.

GeminiMol addresses the problem that functionally similar molecules can appear unrelated in two-dimensional structure. It supports cross-scaffold property prediction, virtual screening, target identification, and scaffold hopping; screening 18 million compounds led to GM-10, an experimentally validated GluN1/GluN3A inhibitor.

2023

Synthetic accessibility

Co-first author

DeepSA

Predicting synthetic accessibility for molecular design.

DeepSA is a co-developed deep-learning model that estimates how difficult a small molecule is to synthesize. Its scores help filter computationally proposed candidates before experimental follow-up and complement other assessments during molecular design.

02 · Interactions — structure & recognition

2022

PPI and molecule glue

First author

PPI-Miner

Finding receptor-compatible backbone motifs beyond sequence similarity.

PPI-Miner reveals interaction partners that sequence-motif searches can miss because unrelated proteins may preserve the local backbone geometry required for receptor recognition. In the CRBN case, it produced 1,739 candidates and recovered 16 previously reported cases; at least 12 additional candidates in the released library later received compound-dependent recruitment support.

Paper ↗ Code ↗ Science ↗

2026

RNA–ligand recognition

Co-first & co-corresponding author

CoCoBind

Predicting whether and where small molecules bind RNA.

CoCoBind treats RNA–compound interaction prediction and nucleotide-level binding-site localization as one multitask problem. Consistency-contrastive learning links the two tasks, supporting site-aware RNA–ligand recognition under distribution shift.

Paper ↗ Code ↗

2026

Protein conformations

Co-corresponding author

ProteinConformers

Evaluating simulated protein conformational landscapes.

Protein simulations generate many structures, making it difficult to judge whether an entire conformational landscape is both diverse and physically plausible. ProteinConformers is a collaborative benchmark dataset, published at NeurIPS, for evaluating these two properties in simulated protein ensembles.

03 · Phenotypes — phenotype & polypharmacology

2025

Multimodal phenotype

Co-first author

PhenoModel

Aligning molecular graphs with cellular morphology.

Cell morphology offers a complementary view of compound activity. PhenoModel is a collaborative multimodal foundation model that aligns molecular graphs with cellular images for phenotype-based virtual screening; the study applied it to identify potential inhibitors of multiple cancer cell types.
