Advanced Science · 2026

Ouroboros

Ouroboros addresses a persistent divide in AI-driven drug discovery: representations optimized for prediction are rarely reusable for molecular design. By organizing chemical space with fingerprint, conformational-space, and pharmacophore relationships, it supports property prediction, virtual screening, and property-guided generation within one representation. This creates a common basis for hit discovery, lead optimization, and multi-target design.

Research progression

From recognizing cross-scaffold similarity to designing within it.

GeminiMol showed that conformational-space and pharmacophore relationships can reveal functionally related molecules across scaffold boundaries. Ouroboros extends this concept into a shared chemical space that connects prediction with candidate design.

2024

Molecular representation

GeminiMol

Finding functional similarity beyond shared 2D scaffolds.

GeminiMol addresses the problem that functionally similar molecules can appear unrelated in two-dimensional structure. It supports cross-scaffold property prediction, virtual screening, target identification, and scaffold hopping; screening 18 million compounds led to GM-10, an experimentally validated GluN1/GluN3A inhibitor.

2022

PPI and molecule glue

PPI-Miner

Finding receptor-compatible backbone motifs beyond sequence similarity.

PPI-Miner reveals interaction partners that sequence-motif searches can miss because unrelated proteins may preserve the local backbone geometry required for receptor recognition. In the CRBN case, it produced 1,739 candidates and recovered 16 previously reported cases; at least 12 additional candidates in the released library later received compound-dependent recruitment support.

News

Selected milestones from collaborative research, software releases, competitions, and publications, including the archive from the earlier site.

  1. 01 / 2026

    The Ouroboros study, Learned Conformational Space and Pharmacophore Into Molecular Foundational Model, was published in Advanced Science.

  2. 09 / 2025

    The PhenoModel study on multimodal phenotypic drug design was published in Acta Pharmaceutica Sinica B.

  3. 11 / 2024

    The PhenoScreen project introduced a dual-space contrastive learning framework combining conformational-space and molecular-image similarities for virtual screening.

  4. 09 / 2024

    A collaborative study using GeminiMol reported a GluN1/GluN3A inhibitor (IC50 = 0.98 μM); the project received first prize in the 2023 Shanghai International Computational Biology Innovation Competition.

  5. 08 / 2024

    The GeminiMol paper was published in Advanced Science.

  7. 10 / 2023

    DeepSA was published in the Journal of Cheminformatics; the DeepSA web server is also available.

  8. 01 / 2023

    DeepSA, co-developed with Shihang Wang, was released as a model for estimating the synthetic accessibility of small molecules.

  9. 12 / 2022

    The original plmd scripts were expanded and renamed AutoMD, adding support for additional force fields and configurable trajectory-analysis pipelines.

  10. 11 / 2022

    The PPI-Miner study showed how conserved backbone motifs can reveal protein-interaction candidates even when their sequences diverge. The paper appeared in the Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling, with source code on GitHub.

  11. 08 / 2022

    A retrospective cross-reference of the released CRBN candidate database found at least 12 candidates beyond the paper's original set of 16 that were later supported by compound-dependent CRBN-recruitment assays; mutating the predicted G-loop glycine abolished the recruitment signal (Science, 2025).

  12. 08 / 2021

    A collaborative study reported an allosteric pocket on the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein and inhibitor candidates designed to restrict its conformational change; the paper appeared in the Journal of Medicinal Chemistry.

  13. 08 / 2021

    A collaborative study reported five candidate small-molecule blockers targeting the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein in Acta Pharmacologica Sinica.

  14. 05 / 2021

    Automated Schrödinger workflows for cross-docking (XDock), virtual screening (GVSrun), and molecular-dynamics simulation (plmd) were released, with source code on GitHub.

  15. 05 / 2020

    Biodb-Search was released as a browser-based navigator that submits one query to multiple biomedical databases.

  16. 12 / 2019

    GetPDB was released as a script for downloading single-chain protein structures by UniProt ID.
