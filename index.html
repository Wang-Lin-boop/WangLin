Featured Research Line · GeminiMol (Adv. Sci. 2024) → Ouroboros (Adv. Sci. 2026) · First Author · Open Code & Weights

Conformational-Space Foundation & Ouroboros Orthogonal Architecture

Across GeminiMol and Ouroboros, we advance two distinct pillars for medicinal chemistry: (1) a conformational-space representation foundation encoding 3D ensembles and pharmacophore geometry to recognize cross-scaffold functional equivalence; and (2) architectural orthogonality in Ouroboros, where representation pretraining, downstream property predictors, and SMILES reconstruction train independently and compose freely at inference time for screening and directed lead optimization.

01 · Chemical Prior

Conformational space & polypharmacology

Flexible drugs such as Imatinib adopt distinct 3D conformations to engage multiple targets (NMOR2, ABL, DDR1, PPARG). Encoding conformational ensembles and pharmacophores overcomes 2D fingerprint blindness.

02 · GeminiMol → Ouroboros

From pairwise CSS to similarity-matrix alignment

GeminiMol pioneered pairwise contrastive pretraining on conformational-space (CSS) and MCS similarity; Ouroboros scales this to full batch similarity-matrix MSE alignment across conformations, pharmacophores, and fingerprints.

03 · Prospective Discovery

Validated across 18M & 48.2M libraries

GeminiMol discovered scaffold-distinct GluN1/GluN3A NAM GM-10 (0.98 μM; 1st/142 teams). Ouroboros navigated 48.2M Enamine compounds via Pictilisib + AT-9283 profiles to uncover multi-target Compound #13.

Compound #13: AURKA 0.42 μM · PI3Kγ 1.74 μM · PI3Kα 8.05 μM

01 · Representation Orthogonality

Train once, project across all tasks

Unlike end-to-end fine-tuning that forks encoder weights for every assay, Ouroboros keeps one frozen chemical representation while lightweight predictors train independently on each property.

02 · Decoupled Generation

Independent SMILES reconstruction

The Transformer Decoder and Token Projector train solely to invert the frozen embedding back to SMILES, cleanly separating molecular generation capacity from assay-specific data scarcity.

03 · Plug-and-Play Composition

Multi-objective optimization on demand

Because predictors and reconstructor share the exact same frozen coordinates, any subset of QSAR, ADME/Tox, similarity, or SA models can be plugged in on the fly to steer hit vectors into new leads.

Stage 1 · Pharmacological Hypothesis

Property & polypharmacology spaces

Translates intra-family, inter-family, and biased-signaling hypotheses—grounded in reference molecules, omics regulation, cell response, and ADMET—into intersecting target and property subspaces.

Stage 2 · Hit Discovery

Multimodal representation & navigation

Fuses conformational space, phenotype, knowledge priors, and chemical language to drive navigation modules (QSAR, ADME/Tox, similarity, SA, safety/kinome, DTI) across commercial chemical space.

Stage 3 · Hit-to-Lead Optimization

Latent iteration & broader chemical decoding

Iteratively updates a hit's 1D representation vector toward multi-objective profiles and decodes novel leads via a Transformer + Token Projector trained over a wider reconstruction chemical space.

Ouroboros

2026 · Advanced Science · First Author

Orthogonal molecular foundation framework decoupling similarity-matrix representation pretraining, independent property predictors, and Transformer SMILES reconstruction so modules compose freely for screening and directed lead optimization.

  • Multi-conformational screening of 48.2M Enamine compounds from Pictilisib + AT-9283 profiles.
  • Compound #13 validated with AURKA IC₅₀ = 0.42 μM, PI3Kγ = 1.74 μM, and PI3Kα = 8.05 μM.

CoCoBind

2026 · J. Med. Chem. · Co-First & Co-Corr. (#*)

Consistency-contrastive multitask learning combining molecular conformation and RNA language representations for joint RNA–ligand interaction scoring and nucleotide-level binding-site identification.

  • Jointly predicts RNA–small-molecule binding probability P(interaction) and residue-level pocket probabilities P(siteᵢ) from RNA sequence and Drug SMILES.
  • Aligns conformational chemical priors with RNA language representations for interpretable RNA-targeted drug discovery.

GeminiMol

2024 · Advanced Science · First Author

Contrastive WLN graph encoder pretrained on conformational-space similarity (CSS) and maximum common substructure (MCS), capturing 3D functional equivalence across scaffolds for zero-shot ligand-based and phenotypic virtual screening.

  • Outperforms classical 2D fingerprints on scaffold-hopping benchmarks, target identification, and ADMET/phenotypic property prediction.
  • Led GeminiMol team to 1st Place / 142 teams in the inaugural Shanghai International Computational Biology Competition.

GluN1/GluN3A NAM Discovery (GM-10)

2025 · Acta Pharmacol. Sin. · Co-First (#)

Prospective ligand-similarity drug screening campaign applying GeminiMol to an 18M-compound commercial library from reference modulator WZB117 to discover a structurally novel GluN1/GluN3A NMDA receptor negative allosteric modulator.

  • Whole-cell patch-clamp electrophysiology validated hit GM-10 with IC₅₀ = 0.98 ± 0.13 μM against GluN1/GluN3A.
  • Achieved true scaffold hopping: 2D fingerprint similarities all < 0.21 vs. GeminiMol similarity 0.678.

MCR-1 Variant & Antimicrobial Peptide

2023 · PLoS Biology · Co-First (#)

Full-length membrane structural modeling and molecular dynamics simulations elucidated how a novel MCR-1 variant mediates Lipid A recognition and colistin/β-lactam co-resistance while revealing a candidate antimicrobial peptide.

  • Mapped Lipid A binding across MCR-1 transmembrane, linker, and catalytic domains (K204, K211, R190, Y200) and conformational free-energy landscapes.
  • Explained cross-resistance phenotypes and identified a derivative peptide with potent antimicrobial activity.

PPI-Miner

2022 · J. Chem. Inf. Model. · First Author

Structure- and sequence-motif co-driven mining engine that identifies receptor-compatible binding interfaces across sequence-divergent proteins, with dedicated workflows for CRBN β-hairpin G-loop molecular-glue neosubstrates.

  • Proteome-wide CRBN scan identified 1,739 G30 candidates and recovered 16 previously reported substrates.
  • Retrospective cross-check with Petzold et al. (Science, 2025) confirmed ≥12 additional released-library hits (WEE1, SCYL1, CSNK1D, etc.).
