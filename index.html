Ouroboros · Model overview

Ouroboros combines a shared molecular encoding with task-specific prediction and SMILES-decoding modules.

  1. 01 · Encode

    Molecular representation

    A GNN encodes molecular graphs as 2048-dimensional vectors; training uses fingerprint, conformational-space, and pharmacophore similarity matrices.

  2. 02 · Navigate

    Property guidance

    Task-specific property decoders score molecular encodings and define objectives used during latent-space search.

  3. 03 · Decode

    Structure generation

    An autoregressive Transformer reconstructs candidate SMILES from input or modified encodings; the candidates require downstream evaluation.

Advanced Science · 2026

Ouroboros

Ouroboros investigates whether chemically informed molecular representations can be reused for both prediction and generation. Its similarity-matrix objective integrates molecular fingerprints, conformational space, and pharmacophore relationships. Separate property decoders and an autoregressive SMILES decoder are used for the tasks evaluated in the study.

Method lineage

From pairwise similarity to similarity matrices.

GeminiMol and Ouroboros both incorporate conformational-space and pharmacophore similarity. GeminiMol learns pairwise relationships, whereas Ouroboros extends supervision to full similarity matrices within each batch.

GeminiMol

Pairwise-similarity contrastive learning

GeminiMol learns from paired molecules, bringing encodings closer when their conformational and pharmacophore profiles are similar.

Evaluated in similarity search and ligand-based virtual screening.

Ouroboros

Matrix-similarity contrastive learning

Ouroboros supervises batches with full molecular similarity matrices, allowing multiple relative relationships to organize the encoding space together.

Evaluated in property prediction, guided generation, and molecular-discovery case studies.

01 · Encode

Represent molecular similarity

A global-attention GNN maps each molecular graph to a 2048-dimensional vector. Training against molecular similarity matrices integrates fingerprint, conformational-space, and pharmacophore relationships.

02 · Navigate

Guide changes in the encoding

Relevant property decoders translate encodings into task-specific predictions and provide objectives for navigating the representation space.

03 · Decode

Decode candidate structures

An autoregressive Transformer reconstructs SMILES from input or revised encodings, yielding candidate structures for subsequent prioritization and evaluation.

Representation model → computational generation workflow

A specified molecular objective can guide search in the learned encoding space.

The workflow begins with a measurable objective, such as a predicted property or similarity to a reference molecule. A selected decoder or similarity function scores encodings during search. The SMILES decoder then converts revised encodings into candidate structures that require further computational filtering and experimental assessment.

  1. 01

    Design question

    A desired profile, reference molecule, phenotype, or target combination defines the search context.

  2. 02

    Computational objective

    A property decoder or similarity function supplies the score used during search.

  3. 03

    Encoding-space navigation

    Search or iterative updates move molecular vectors toward a selected computational objective.

  4. 04

    Structure reconstruction

    The decoder returns candidate SMILES for downstream computational and experimental assessment.

Evaluated search settings

Within the study's formulation, a selected objective can be used in several complementary search settings.

Chemical-space search

For virtual screening or exploratory generation, predicted scores rank or guide candidates sampled from chemical space.

Hit-to-lead optimization

Starting from a known hit, the method iteratively updates its encoding while tracking similarity to the starting structure and selected predicted properties.

Multi-reference navigation

Several reference sets or objectives can be combined to propose candidates for a multi-target hypothesis; these remain computational proposals pending validation.
